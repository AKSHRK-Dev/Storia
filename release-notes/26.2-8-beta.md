## New: standby Storia Relay

A second relay can now run as a **standby**: it keeps a live copy of everything the active relay stores, and takes
over with `promote` if the active relay's machine or disk is lost. Design: `STANDBY.md`.

- **No confirmed write is lost.** While the standby is in sync, a worker is told "stored" only once the write is on
  both relays. Writes the active relay never confirmed are sent again by the worker to the new active relay.
- **A slow or stopped standby never stops the cluster**: after 2 s without an answer the active relay goes on alone
  and the standby copies the world again when it is back.
- **Manual take-over**: `promote` on the standby refuses while the active relay still runs, and refuses if the
  standby was not in sync (`promote force` overrides). The old relay, started again, becomes the standby by itself.
- Workers (`cluster.coordinator`) and Storia Proxy (`[cluster] coordinator`) accept a list of relays,
  `"relay-a:25590,relay-b:25590"`, and connect to whichever is active.
- Settings in `relay.properties`: `role=active|standby` and `peer=<the other relay>`.

Tested: the active relay killed (kill -9) during a 1,521-chunk pregeneration, then `promote`: every chunk and every
block placed before the kill is on the new relay; the standby restarted during heavy writes and caught up; the old
relay came back as the standby and both worlds were identical, record for record; Storia Proxy moved to the new
relay by itself.

## Also in this release

- **Cluster workers load and save chunks on 8 threads** (when `chunk-system.io-threads` is left at -1). Their chunk
  I/O goes over the network, so overlapping the round trips helps: pregenerating 2,601 chunks on one worker went
  from 88 s to 59 s.
- **A worker keeps its index (and its entity ids) when it reconnects** to a relay that restarted or took over. Before,
  two or more workers reconnecting in a different order could get each other's index and fail to reconnect.
