## What's new since 26.2

### Storia Cluster: one world on several servers

Several Storia servers can now run **one world together**. Each **Storia Worker** runs the part of the world
where its players are; **Storia Relay** stores the world and decides which worker runs which part; players move
between workers through **Storia Proxy without a loading screen**, keeping their inventory, advancements and
statistics.

- Players who come close are put on the same worker before they could see each other's chunks; busy workers hand
  groups to quiet ones. Contraptions on a border between two parts are found and always run on one worker.
- Time, weather, game rules, the scoreboard, maps, advancements and statistics are shared.
- `/stop` on a worker moves its players to the others first. If the relay is away for a while, workers keep
  running and keep their writes on disk until it is back. A player whose data cannot be read is not let in, so
  their saved data is never overwritten.
- A new worker needs no copy of the world: it fetches the world settings from the relay on first start.
- Checked with a real Minecraft 26.2 client: moves by balancing, by flying into another worker's area and by
  `/stop` keep the connection and the world on screen.

Guide: https://storiamc.com/en-us/docs/cluster/ · Worker setup: https://storiamc.com/en-us/docs/worker/

Seamless moves need Minecraft 26.1/26.2 clients. Plugin data is not shared between workers yet.

### Busy spawns stay smooth: tick guard

When a region gets too slow, mobs in crowded chunks think less often (sensing, targets, brains); they still move,
path and collide every tick, and redstone is never touched. Players standing nearby are not limited. On a test
spawn with 1,400 mobs the region went from 50–55 ms to 32–35 ms per tick (TPS 20).

### Player budget limits only fast movers

While an area is busy, only players moving faster than 12 blocks/s (elytra, ...) get a shorter view distance.
Players who stand, build or walk are never limited.

### Removed: terrain-only offload

The old Storia Worker that only computed terrain noise for one server (`offload.*` in `storia.yml`,
`/storia offload`) was replaced by the cluster. An `offload:` section in `storia.yml` is ignored. The code is kept
in the `archive/terrain-offload` branch.
