## What is new since 26.2-5

This stable release brings together everything tested in 26.2-6-beta to 26.2-9-beta. When upgrading a Storia
Cluster, update Storia Relay, every worker and Storia Proxy together. No setting has to change: the new features are
off until you turn them on.

### New: Linear region format

Worlds can be stored in the [Linear region format](https://github.com/xymb-endcrystalme/LinearRegionFileFormatTools):
each region is one Zstandard-compressed `r.X.Z.linear` file. Set `region-format.type: linear` in `storia.yml`.

- **About half the disk space**: 12 overworld regions went from 46.1 MB (Anvil) to 25.5 MB at compression level 1
  and 21.4 MB at level 6.
- **Existing worlds just work**: a region is read from its `.mca` the first time it is used and written as `.linear`
  from then on. The `.mca` files are left in place until you delete them.
- **Compatible with the Linear tools** in both directions. Works with the RAM world.
- An unreadable `.linear` file is never overwritten, and Storia refuses to start on `anvil` when the world has
  `.linear` files.

Guide: https://storiamc.com/en-us/docs/linear/

### New: standby Storia Relay

A second relay can run as a **standby** that keeps a live copy of everything the active relay stores, and takes over
with `promote` if the active relay's machine or disk is lost. Set `role=standby` and `peer=<the active relay>` in its
`relay.properties`, and give workers and Storia Proxy both relays: `"relay-a:25590,relay-b:25590"`.

- **No confirmed write is lost**: while the standby is in sync, a worker is told "stored" only once the write is on
  both relays.
- A slow or stopped standby never stops the cluster (the active relay goes on alone after 2 s).
- `promote` refuses while the active relay still runs or when the standby was not in sync (`promote force`
  overrides). The old relay, started again, becomes the standby by itself.

Guide: https://storiamc.com/en-us/docs/relay/#standby

### Faster and lighter Storia Cluster

- **Cluster workers load and save chunks on 8 threads** (when `chunk-system.io-threads` is -1): pregenerating 2,601
  chunks on one worker went from 88 s to 59 s.
- **Chunks are no longer compressed twice** on the encrypted link: Storia Relay uses about 23% less CPU.
- The relay remembers where players were, also after a restart.

### Fixes

- **No lost writes when the relay cannot store them** (a full disk, for example): writes are kept and sent again;
  one still refused after 10 minutes is moved to `cluster-spool/failed/` instead of being deleted.
- Scoreboard changes made while the relay is away wait, in order, instead of being dropped.
- A worker keeps its index (its range of entity ids) when the relay restarts or another relay takes over.
- Time and weather reports no longer pile up while the relay is slow.
- Contraption links respect the hand-over pause.
- Storia Relay closes connections that never say who they are after 10 seconds, and writes the world to disk on stop.
- Clearer error messages in the plugin API.

Tested before this release: a single server; two workers with bots (moves, merging, balancing, `/stop`); a relay
outage and a relay that refuses writes (no write lost); a worker that cannot read the world; scoreboard, time,
weather and game rules; shared plugin data (400 of 400 concurrent increments); the active relay killed during
pregeneration and the standby promoted (every chunk kept, both worlds identical afterwards); Linear files read and
written by the Linear tools.

All release notes: https://storiamc.com/en-us/releases/
